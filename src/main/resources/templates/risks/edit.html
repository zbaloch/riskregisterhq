Residual Risk (After Measures)

The remaining risk level after controls and mitigation measures are applied.

Risk Treatment Decision

Risk Treatment Options:

  • Awaiting Assessment: Risk identified, pending review to decide treatment
  • Accept: Acknowledge the residual risk and monitor it regularly
  • Mitigate: Implement additional controls to further reduce the risk
  • Transfer: Shift the risk through insurance, contracts, or outsourcing
  • Avoid: Eliminate the activity or process that creates the risk
