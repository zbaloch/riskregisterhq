Risk Effectiveness Score

What It Measures

This score shows how effectively your controls reduce risk across all approved risks.

Calculation Formula

Effectiveness = (1 - Total Residual / Total Inherent) × 100%

Total Inherent: Sum of (Likelihood × Impact) for all approved risks

Total Residual: Sum of (Residual Likelihood × Residual Impact) for approved risks

Example

Risk 1: Inherent = 5×5 = 25, Residual = 2×2 = 4

Risk 2: Inherent = 4×4 = 16, Residual = 2×3 = 6

Total Inherent: 25 + 16 = 41

Total Residual: 4 + 6 = 10

Effectiveness: (1 - 10/41) × 100% = 75.6%

Score Interpretation

80-100%: Excellent

Controls are highly effective

50-79%: Good

Controls provide solid risk reduction

<50%: Needs Improvement

Consider additional controls
